# Catch on your G2: the touch guide

Catch stays out of your way. Most of the time the lens shows **one dim dot** in the top-right corner, and nothing else. You only touch the glasses when you want something back.

## What you see

| On the lens | What it means |
|---|---|
| A dim **dot**, top right | Catch is listening. All is well. |
| **No dot** at all | Catch can't hear right now (check the phone's network, or open Catch in the Even app). |
| `Priya` (one dim line, low on the lens) | Someone just told you their name. It fades after a second and a half. |
| `promised · send Priya the deck tonight` | You just promised something. Catch is holding it for you. |
| `Dev promised · sends the agenda` | Someone promised **you** something. |
| `…and the second reason is` | You were cut off mid-sentence. These were your last words. They stay until you speak again or tap. |

## The touches

The G2's touchpad is on the side of the glasses. Every touch below is a single, simple move.

| Touch | Where | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **Tap** | at the dot | Shows the **names** in this conversation (for five seconds). |
| **Tap** | on a line or a page | Makes it go away. |
| **Swipe down** | on the names page | Shows the next names, then **your promises** (`this conversation 2/2`). |
| **Swipe up** | on a page | Goes back one page. |
| **Swipe up** | at the dot | Asks `keep this conversation?` |
| **Tap** | on `keep this conversation?` | **Keeps** it (`kept`). After it ends you get a short summary, the *jogger*, on your phone. |
| **Swipe down** | on `keep this conversation?` | No thanks. Nothing is kept. |
| **Swipe down** | at the bare dot | **Ends the conversation** (`conversation ended`). Its names and promises go to your phone. |

**To start a conversation,** just talk. With Catch open, your own voice starts a new conversation.

## Two touches that belong to Even, not Catch

| Touch | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Long-press** | Even asks *"end this feature?"*. Answer **No** to keep Catch running. (**Yes** closes Catch.) |
| **Double-tap** | Closes Catch. |

## On your phone

Open **Catch** in the Even app. Its page shows your conversations, newest first. The list refreshes by itself every few seconds.

- **A conversation shows up** once it has ended, and only if it has a promise or you kept it. (Nothing else is saved: that's the privacy promise.)
- **Tap a conversation** to see its names, **YOU** (your promises) and **OWED TO YOU**.
- **Tick a promise** when it's done.
- **Add a name or a promise** by hand, at the bottom of a conversation.
- **Kept conversation ›** opens its jogger: the key points, in order, with technical terms in bold.
- **End conversation** (at the top of the list) does the same as a swipe down at the dot.
- **Delete conversation** (at the bottom of one) removes it everywhere, after a confirm.

## Quick tips

- **Keep Catch open** in the Even app, with the phone unlocked. Even Hub apps only run while they're open.
- **Speak naturally.** Catch tells your voice from others' using the glasses' own microphones.
- **Nothing showing?** Catch is meant to stay dark. A slip is the only thing that lights it.
