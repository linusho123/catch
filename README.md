# Catch

**Catch holds what you were about to lose.** It is an Even Hub app for the Even Realities G2 that stays dark until a conversation slips: the name someone just told you, the thing you just promised, or the sentence you were in the middle of when someone cut you off. Then it shows one dim line, and lets it go.

## For judges and testers

**Install (G2 + iPhone):** you need the Even app 2.2.10 or later with a G2 paired. Send us the email of your Even account; we add it to Catch's Beta group in Even Hub. Accept the invite, install **Catch** from Even Hub in the Even app, open it, and allow the glasses microphone. No account or key of your own is needed: the hosted server (`wss://catch-g2.fly.dev`) and its token are built into the package.

**What you will see:**
- A dim dot, top right: Catch is listening. No dot means it cannot hear (check the phone's network).
- Someone says "Hi, I'm Priya" → `Priya`, dim, lower third, for a second and a half.
- You say "I'll send you the link tonight" → `promised · send … the link tonight`.
- Someone else says "I'll send you the agenda" → `Dev promised · send the agenda` (the last name caught; `they promised · …` before anyone is named, and the next name caught attaches to that promise).
- You stop mid-sentence and someone talks over you for two seconds or more → your last words (`…and the second reason is`) wait until you speak again or tap.

**Controls:** tap shows the names held in this conversation (five seconds, or tap again); swipe down from there shows your promises, then those owed to you (`owed: Dev sends the agenda`); swipe up asks `keep this conversation?` and a tap keeps it (a kept conversation gets a short jogger of its key points on the phone page when it ends); long-press ends the conversation now (ten minutes of your silence also ends it); double-tap is Even's exit. Catch tells your voice from others' by loudness on the simulator and by the glasses' own speaker role on a G2, so the person introducing themselves should be a step or two away.

**On the phone:** Catch's own page in the Even app lists past conversations that produced a promise or were kept, newest first, with each conversation's names, your promises (YOU), the promises owed to you (OWED TO YOU), and the jogger of a kept conversation.

**Privacy:** nothing survives a conversation unless it produced a promise or you kept it; the transcript and audio are deleted when it ends, a kept one's once its jogger is written (D76). Speech-to-text is Deepgram with model-improvement opted out; the promise detector sends sentences with a commitment in them, yours and other people's, and a few before them, and a kept conversation's transcript (for its jogger) to Gemini on the free tier, under which Google may use that content to improve its models.

**Built at LA Hacks:** the spec (`features/`, `docs/BUILD-PLAN.md`) was written before the event; every line of code was written during it, through a reviewed build pipeline. Not built: tick-off on the phone, a website, phone push notifications, pause, and restart persistence (see `features/OUT-OF-SCOPE.md`).

**Run it without glasses:** see "Run Catch on this machine" below (the Even Hub simulator plus a local server).

## Run Catch on this machine (D128)

Nothing needs Fly. Copy `.env.example` to `.env` in `catch/` (untracked) and fill in `DEEPGRAM_API_KEY` and `CATCH_AUDIO_TOKEN` (any long random string; it is the shared token `/audio` demands). Then, in two terminals:

    npm run local          # the server on ws://localhost:8080, the lens dev server on http://localhost:5173 pointed at it
    npm run lens:simulate  # the Even Hub simulator on the lens, with the laptop mic as the glasses mic

`npm run local` reads `.env` with Node's `--env-file-if-exists`, starts `server/src/main.ts`, and starts Vite with `VITE_CATCH_SERVER=ws://localhost:8080` and `VITE_CATCH_TOKEN` set to the same `CATCH_AUDIO_TOKEN`, so the two always match. The only network it needs is Deepgram. The simulator shows the dim dot top right once the server's Deepgram stream is open (no dot means Catch cannot hear: no key, no network, or the server is down); when someone else says "Hi, I'm Priya", `Priya` shows in the lower third for a second and a half, and a click (tap) shows the names page (`this conversation 1/2`, the names in the order heard, `↓ promises` at the foot) for five seconds, or `this conversation · nothing held`. With `GEMINI_API_KEY` in `.env`, say "I'll send you the deck tonight": `promised · send … the deck tonight` shows for a second and a half, and a swipe down (scroll down) on the names page shows the promises page (`this conversation 2/2`, or `no promises yet`); when someone else says "I'll send you the agenda", `Dev promised · send the agenda` shows (Dev being the last name caught, or `they` before any), and the promises page lists it after yours as `owed: Dev sends the agenda`. Stop mid-sentence and let someone else talk for two seconds: your last words (`…and the second reason is`) wait in the lower third until you speak again or click. A long-press ends the conversation; so do ten minutes in which the wearer says nothing. The simulator's double-click is Even's exit: it closes Catch, it is not a tap. Off the glasses the split is loudness (D126): the wearer is whoever is loud at the mic, so the person introducing themselves should speak from a step or two away. On a laptop mic the server's own threshold (-20 dBFS) puts the wearer's normal voice under it, so `npm run local` sets `CATCH_LOUDNESS_THRESHOLD_DBFS` to -25 when `.env` leaves it unset (on a MacBook Air, -25 told the wearer at about 30 cm from another voice at about 2 m); set it in `.env` to tune it (`npm run local` prints the value in use). The server's default and the test fixtures stay at -20. The lens page opened in a plain browser tab (`http://localhost:5173` in Safari, to look at the phone page) has no Even bridge, so it shows the phone page only and never opens `/audio`: it cannot take the simulator's session (its audio and its dot) away. `http://localhost:5173/glyphs.html` in the simulator is the glyph check. The simulator does not hold the lens to `app.json`'s network whitelist (checked 2026-10-17, simulator 0.9.5): `ws://localhost:8080` is not in it and connects.

## Even Beta: the package and installing it (testers)

`npm run lens:pack` builds `catch.ehpk` in `catch/`, always pointed at the hosted server `wss://catch-g2.fly.dev` (the one origin in `app.json`), whatever a local `.env` says. It builds the shared token in from `VITE_CATCH_TOKEN`: put `VITE_CATCH_TOKEN=<the CATCH_AUDIO_TOKEN value>` in the untracked `apps/lens/.env.local` first; without it `lens:pack` stops before building, naming `VITE_CATCH_TOKEN` and `apps/lens/.env.local`. `CATCH_ALLOW_NO_TOKEN=1 npm run lens:pack` builds one without the token, only to check that the package builds: the server refuses it, so never upload that one. The `.ehpk` is never committed. Uploading it to Even Hub as a Beta build and managing the Beta group are the operator's (Even Hub's developer site); there is no loaner G2, so the phone-side checks (lock and resume, permission denial, double-tap exit) are run by the operator once a G2 is in hand.

What a tester does to install Catch from the Beta:

1. Have an iPhone with the Even app 2.2.10 or later, signed in to an Even account, with G2 paired to it.
2. Send the operator the email of that Even account; the operator adds it to Catch's Beta group in Even Hub.
3. Accept the invitation, then install **Catch** from Even Hub in the Even app and open it. Allow the glasses microphone when asked.
4. No account or key of your own is needed: the shared token is inside the package. The hosted server must be up (`fly status -a catch-g2`, the operator's).

Using it: a dim dot top right means Catch is listening (no dot: it cannot hear, so check the phone's network). When someone says "Hi, I'm Priya", `Priya` shows dimly in the lower third for a second and a half. If you stop mid-sentence and someone else talks over you for two seconds, your last words (`…and the second reason is`) wait in the lower third until you speak again or tap. Tap to see the names held in this conversation (five seconds, or tap again); swipe up to keep the conversation (`keep this conversation?` waits for a tap to keep it, `kept`, or a swipe down to decline; `already kept` once it is), and its memory jogger reaches the phone page when it ends; long-press to end the conversation; double-tap is Even's exit. The split is loudness (D126): the person introducing themselves should be a step or two away, quieter than you.

## Server and Fly

Server: `npm run server` locally (reads `.env` the same way; `PORT`, `CATCH_DB`, `CATCH_SPLIT`, `CATCH_LOUDNESS_THRESHOLD_DBFS` (-20 dBFS when unset; `npm run local` makes it -25), the optional `CATCH_WEARER_NAME` (the wearer's name, never caught, whatever the split decides), the optional `CATCH_THREAD_DETECTION` (`on` by default; `off` stops the dropped-thread line coming on its own, and a tap at the dot then shows the wearer's last words at the top of the held page, D120: the operator's call after tuning, never set by the build), the optional `GEMINI_API_KEY` (the promise detector; without it Catch promises nothing; Catch's key is on the Gemini free tier, under which Google may use the content sent, here the sentences with a commitment in them, the wearer's and other people's, and the few sentences before them, to improve its models), and the required `CATCH_AUDIO_TOKEN`, the shared token `/audio` demands; the lens builds in the same value as `VITE_CATCH_TOKEN`). A conversation's promises, the wearer's and those owed to the wearer, reach the phone through the phone page's history (there is no phone push yet); on Fly, `fly deploy` from this directory (`fly.toml`, `Dockerfile`; the store on the `catch_data` volume at `/data/catch.db`; secrets `DEEPGRAM_API_KEY`, `GEMINI_API_KEY`, `CATCH_AUDIO_TOKEN`, optionally `CATCH_WEARER_NAME`). The lanes never reach the network: the real provider and the real model run only by hand, behind `CATCH_REAL_STT=1` (the harness gives the server Deepgram, plays a fixture's recorded `<name>.wav` in real time, and fails loudly on the synthetic fixtures, which have none) and `CATCH_REAL_LLM=1` (records cassettes).
