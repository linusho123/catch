# User needs — Catch

The needs ledger for Catch, reconciled from the whole interview record against the intent-checkpoint baseline (N0–N7, ratified D3–D10) on 2026-10-15. Every need is an N entry in `DOCKET.md`. The ledger explains and prioritizes; it never specifies. At build, only the feature files bind: a `partial` status is a ratified stopping point, not debt, and a builder who believes the ledger implies missing behavior raises the question rather than building from the ledger.

Beneficiaries: **user** is the wearer (the visionary is also the primary wearer); **team** is the four-person hackathon team building and demoing Catch.

## Needs

Evidence: D1, D2, D11, D64, D65, D82, D93, D94, D96, D97, D101, D107, D108 — the boundary rulings that shaped the interview. Coverage: `scenario` — every feature file; the vision is the sum of N1–N5 and N8.
