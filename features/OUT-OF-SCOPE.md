## Named assumptions

- **Others cannot see the lens.** Even states the display is visible only to the wearer with almost zero light leakage; reviewers report at most a faint off-axis glint at high brightness. No scenario asserts what others see; Catch's only lever is to keep every catch dim. Reopens if a judge or reviewer reports reading a catch from across the table. (D15, 2026-10-15)
- **Overheard names are held.** Catch cannot tell a nearby conversation from the wearer's own, so an introduction overheard from someone the wearer is not talking to is held like any other name. Reopens if the names page is routinely polluted in ordinary use. (D44, 2026-10-15)
- **The usability limits are accepted.** People whose names or accents speech-to-text gets wrong, and people new to the touch vocabulary, get nothing extra; a quick user guide is the whole answer for the second. Reopens if a user guide proves insufficient at the demo. (D72, 2026-10-15)
- **The team is trusted as operator.** For the hackathon the team may read the kept joggers and promise lists it hosts. Reopens on any use beyond the hackathon. (D77, 2026-10-15)
- **Consent is the wearer's job.** Catch gives the people the wearer is with no sign that they are heard, and consent, where the law requires it, is the wearer's responsibility, as with any recorder. Reopens on public release or any use beyond the event. (D85, 2026-10-15)
- **A repeated name is decided by timing, not identity.** A repeat within two minutes is a second person; later is a re-introduction. Catch cannot tell voices apart. Reopens if voice identification ever becomes available. (D87, D59 reversed by D87, 2026-10-15)

## Out of reach by construction — stated so nobody expects it

- **Speaker identification.** Catch knows the wearer's voice from everyone else's by loudness alone and never knows which other person is speaking; every ruling that names another person rests on a name having been caught. (D95, 2026-10-15)
- **Anything said while Catch cannot hear or is paused.** No catch, no held item and no jogger line comes from those minutes; a kept jogger says only how long was missed. (D37, D86, D53, 2026-10-15)
